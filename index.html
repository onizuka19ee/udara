Updates & privacy

Readings are retrieved by this site’s scheduled GitHub workflow. Refresh checks the latest published copy; it does not trigger a new government measurement. Updates may be delayed. Readings older than 3 hours are marked outdated. Your coordinates are used only in your browser to find the nearest Malaysian station; they are not stored or sent to this site’s server. Your selected station is remembered on this device. Loading a map connects to OpenStreetMap and shares the selected station’s coordinates, not your exact position.
